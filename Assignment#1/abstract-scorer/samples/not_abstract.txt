Preheat the oven to 220 degrees Celsius and line a baking tray with parchment paper. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and a pinch of salt. Cut the cold butter into small cubes and rub it into the flour mixture with your fingertips until it resembles coarse breadcrumbs. Pour in the buttermilk and stir gently with a fork until the dough just comes together; do not overmix or the scones will be tough. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface, pat it into a round about three centimeters thick, and cut out circles with a cutter. Place the scones on the tray, brush the tops with a little milk, and bake for twelve to fifteen minutes until golden brown. Serve warm with clotted cream and strawberry jam.
